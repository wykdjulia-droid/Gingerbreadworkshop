How to build it
  1. Print at 100% / actual size on thick paper or thin card.
  2. Cut along the solid lines — keep the little glue tabs!
  3. Fold along every dashed line (score with a ruler first for crisp edges).
  4. Glue the long end tab behind the front wall to close the box.
  5. Fold the roof over and glue it onto the triangle tabs and the top tab.
  6. Fold the bottom tabs inwards and glue the base piece onto them to close the bottom.
  7. Pop it on a plate and add cotton-wool snow ❄
