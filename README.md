# 🎄 Gingerbread Workshop

Decorate your very own gingerbread house, then print a fold-up paper net of your design and build it for real!

## What you can do

- **Decorate every side** of the house in a live 3D preview — front, back, both sides and both roof panels.
- **Press on sweets:** gumdrops, peppermints, candy canes, heart and star cookies, windows (arched, square, round, tall), doors (arched, square, fancy), bows, wreaths, trees, gingerbread men, holly, lollipops, snowmen, choc buttons and icing dots.
- **Pipe icing** as smooth lines or little beads, or add a bead border and icing dots in one tap.
- **Choose your walls** (golden, classic or chocolate gingerbread, or mint, pink or cream fondant) and **roof** (iced shingles, icing waves, snowy, sugar tiles, candy stripe or plain).
- **Change the shape** of the house — width, depth, wall height and roof steepness.
- **Print your house** as a PDF or PNG on A4, Letter or A3 paper, with cut lines, fold lines, glue tabs and a base.

## How to build it

1. Print at **100% / actual size** on thick paper or thin card.
2. Cut along the **solid** lines — keep the little glue tabs!
3. Fold along every **dashed** line (score with a ruler first for crisp edges).
4. Glue the long end tab behind the front wall to close the box.
5. Fold the roof over and glue it onto the triangle tabs and the top tab.
6. Fold the bottom tabs inwards and glue the **base** piece onto them.
7. Pop it on a plate and add cotton-wool snow ❄

## Running it

It's a single self-contained web page — just open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on **GitHub Pages** for this repository to share it with a link.

Works on computers, tablets and phones. Happy decorating! ❤️
